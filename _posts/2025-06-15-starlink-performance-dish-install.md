# Starlink Performance Dish Install: Perfect Timing for Our Allures Voyage

*(Live from our X thread @mns_grn—June 28, 2025. Expanded with install deets for fellow offshore hackers.)*

Waited months for a @Starlink dish for our sailing trip, needing 12V like the Mini, power use like the Standard, & performance of the Flat High Performance. Just days before buying, Starlink unveiled their new Performance dish!  

![Starlink Performance dish mounted on Allures 45.9 boom](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GujzVHxWIAApob6.jpg)  

Powering our Starlink dish & router with a single 12V cigarette lighter plug, enabling flawless video calls while drawing ~5 amps. The new Performance dish is all we could hope for! Not in its final spot yet, but worth every penny! Thank you @Starlink !!  

In case somebody wonders, you can power the new dish with anything from 12-57V in DC. A game changer!

![Starlink Performance Power Supply label](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GzJqqVjbcAAf3CG.jpg)
*Power Supply label of the new Performance Gen3 Starlink kit. (Direct from X media.)*

## Quick Install Notes for Expedition Rigs
- **Mount**: Boom-drilled with epoxy backing—Allures aluminum holds like iron. Tilt 20° north; app AR scan cleared rigging obstructions.
- **Power Hack**: Cigarette lighter to PoE (under 100W peak)—ties to Victron batteries. Our wind AI now pulls live GRIBs via this beast.
- **Wins**: 150+Mbps off Gibraltar. Offline mode for AI predictions when sats dip.
